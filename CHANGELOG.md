# Changelog

## 1.0.0, 12 October 2026

First release. Research prototype, not investment advice.

- The [plan](docs/plan.md)'s tests, run once on a [frozen dataset](docs/dataset.md) of 120 events on 79 companies, from 197 Kalshi ladders that Codex checked against the companies' own publications. The [results](docs/results.md) show an accurate market forecast, and leave open whether a beat or a miss moves the share.
- A comparison with 2 simple forecasts, outside the plan: the market came closer than the same quarter a year earlier on 124 of 137 metrics, and closer than the quarter before on 118 of 135 ([how](docs/benchmark.md)).
- Explanations of the 10 largest results-day moves against the surprise, each from news published by the end of results day.
- Codex's forecasts for 11 third-quarter 2026 figures, filed before the reports ([forecasts](replication/README.md)).
- The `mef` command-line tool, which runs and checks every step.
